1. Who we are

Cab Sarathi (the "app", "we", "us") is operated by [Company / owner legal name], [Registered address, City, Uttarakhand – PIN], India. Cab Sarathi is a platform that lets riders connect directly with independent taxi drivers. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you use the Cab Sarathi app, the website cabsarathi.in and related services.

We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them. By using Cab Sarathi you agree to this policy.

2. Data we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your mobile number (used to log in with an OTP) and whether you are a rider or a driver.
  • Profile: name, email (optional), city, gender (optional), profile photo, your preferred language and an emergency contact number for SOS.
  • Driver details: father's name, date of birth, blood group (optional), address, PIN code, state, driving licence and vehicle RC images, and vehicle details (make, type, model, registration number, seats, AC).
  • Trips: ride requests (pickup, destination, date, time, number of people, budget), routes posted by drivers, booking offers, bookings, cancellations and their reasons.
  • Chats: messages and photos you send in chat. If you tap "call request", your phone number is shared with that person.
  • Ratings, reports and support: ratings and reviews, reports about other users, people you block, and messages you send to our support team.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: see section 5.
  • Device: a notification token (to send you push notifications), a random install ID created by the app (to protect your notifications from misuse), app version and language.
  • Logs: IP address, time and type of requests, used for security, preventing abuse and fixing errors.

Payments

Ride fares are paid by the rider directly to the driver (cash or UPI). We do not collect or store any card, bank or UPI details for rides. If a driver buys the optional verified badge, the payment is handled by our payment partner; we receive only the payment status, not your card or bank details.

3. How we use your data

  • To create your account, log you in and keep it secure.
  • To show riders and drivers to each other, let you chat, make bookings and complete rides.
  • To verify drivers' documents and show a "verified" status.
  • To send notifications about chats, bookings, ride updates and account status.
  • For safety: boarding OTPs, trip sharing, SOS, handling reports and blocking misuse.
  • To answer support requests and resolve complaints.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and abuse (for example, rate limits and checks on suspicious activity).
  • To improve the app and fix problems.
  • To comply with the law and respond to valid requests from authorities.

We do not sell your personal data.

Service providers

We use trusted providers who process data only on our instructions:

  • Cloud hosting for our servers and database (located in India).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, to deliver push notifications.
  • An SMS provider, to send login OTPs to your mobile number.
  • Google Maps Platform, for place search and distances, where enabled.
  • An email provider, for support emails.
  • A payment partner, only for the optional driver verified badge.

Legal reasons

We may share information with police or other authorities when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect someone's safety in an emergency.

Emergency contact

When you press SOS or share a trip, the app prepares a message with your ride details and location, and you send it yourself (for example on WhatsApp) to your chosen contact.

5. Location

  • Drivers: when you open nearby ride requests, the app uses your location to show requests within your area. We store your last known location so we can notify you about new requests near you.
  • Riders: places you search for or choose as pickup and destination are used to find drivers and are saved with your request.
  • SOS and trip sharing: your location is added to the message only when you tap SOS or Share.

You can turn off location permission in your phone settings at any time; some features (like nearby requests) will then not work.

6. Advertising

Cab Sarathi may show banner advertisements in the app, chosen by us (for example, from local businesses). These ads are shown based on whether you use the app as a rider or a driver — not on your personal profile — and we do not share your personal data with advertisers. If we ever use a third-party advertising network, we will update this policy and tell you before it starts.

7. How long we keep your data

  • While your account is active: your profile, trips and chats are kept so the app works and you can see your history.
  • Login OTP records: deleted after 7 days.
  • Notifications: deleted after 30 days.
  • Old and cancelled ride requests: removed automatically after they expire.
  • When you delete your account: your account is closed immediately and your phone number is freed. As required under the IT Rules, 2021, we keep your personal information in secure, restricted storage for 180 days (for complaints, safety investigations and legal needs) and then delete it permanently.
  • Shared records: rides and chats also belong to the other person's history, so your name may still appear there after you leave; your phone number never does.

8. Security

  • All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Driving licence and RC images are stored privately and are never available through a public link; only authorised staff can view them for verification.
  • Uploaded photos are checked and re-processed to remove hidden data such as GPS tags.
  • Staff access to the admin panel requires a password and an email code, and every action is logged.

No system is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your data and will inform you and the authorities as required by law if a breach affects you.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account from Profile → Delete account. If you have an ongoing or upcoming ride, complete or cancel it first.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications on or off in your phone settings.
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop using the app and delete your account at any time.
  • Other requests or complaints: contact our Grievance Officer (below). You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is in operation.

10. Children

Cab Sarathi is meant for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. Minors may travel only with a parent or guardian who makes the booking. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. This website

cabsarathi.in does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our web server keeps basic logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. The contact form does not send anything to us directly — it opens your own email app.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app grows. When we make important changes we will notify you in the app or on this page, and update the "Last updated" date above.
