About us

Bringing riders and drivers closer

Cab Sarathi is a simple, honest way to travel between towns — riders talk directly to local taxi drivers, agree a fair fare and travel safely, with no middleman taking a cut.

Our story

Why we built Cab Sarathi

In the hills and small towns, most people still find a taxi by asking around at the stand or calling a number someone shared. Riders never know if the fare is fair, and drivers often run trips with empty seats while paying high commissions on big ride-hailing apps.

We built Cab Sarathi to fix both problems. Drivers post the trips they already make, riders post where they need to go, and both sides talk directly in the app. The fare is agreed openly, the ride is confirmed with an OTP, and the money goes straight to the driver.

स

Why "Sarathi"? In Hindi, sarathi (सारथी) means a charioteer — the trusted one who drives you safely to where you need to go. That is what we want every driver on our app to be.

A fair fare for the rider, the full fare for the driver, and a safe trip for everyone.
— The Cab Sarathi promise

How we stay free

A simple, transparent model

No commission on rides — ever. Here is how Cab Sarathi works for each side.

Always free

For riders

Searching, posting ride requests, chatting with drivers, booking and safety features are completely free. You pay only the fare you agree with the driver, directly to them.

Optional

For drivers

Joining, posting routes and getting riders is free, with zero commission. Drivers who want extra trust can choose a paid verified badge on their profile — it is optional and never required to get rides.

Coming soon

Advertising

We may show a few relevant banner ads in the app — for example from local businesses — to keep the service free. Ads never change who you see or what you pay.
