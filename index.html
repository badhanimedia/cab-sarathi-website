FAQ

Questions? We have answers

How is the fare decided?

Drivers set their own fares, and you can discuss it with them in chat. Cab Sarathi does not add any commission or hidden charges. You pay exactly what you agreed.

How do I pay for my ride?

You pay the driver directly in cash or UPI, as you both agree. No money is charged inside the app.

Are the drivers verified?

Yes. Every driver submits their driving licence, vehicle RC and photo, and our team reviews them before the driver can see riders' requests.

Can I share a ride to save money?

Yes. Many drivers offer seat sharing on their routes. Book only the seats you need and split the cost of the trip.

How do I cancel a booking?

Open the ride in the Rides tab and tap "Cancel ride". The other person is notified immediately.
