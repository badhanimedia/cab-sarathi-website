2. What Cab Sarathi is and isn't

Cab Sarathi is a technology platform that helps riders and independent taxi drivers find each other, chat and agree on trips.

Important: Cab Sarathi does not provide transport services, does not own or operate vehicles and does not employ drivers. Each ride is an agreement directly between the rider and the driver. Drivers are independent and are solely responsible for the transport service they provide.

We act as an intermediary under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and follow the due-diligence requirements of the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

5. Additional terms for riders

  • Using Cab Sarathi as a rider is free. You pay only the fare you agree with the driver.
  • Be at the pickup point on time, respect the driver and the vehicle, and travel with only the number of people and luggage you agreed.
  • Share your boarding OTP only after you are inside the correct vehicle.
  • Do not carry anything illegal or dangerous.

6. Fares, bookings and payment

  • Fares are set by drivers and agreed between the rider and the driver in the app. A booking is confirmed when the rider accepts the driver's booking offer.
  • The rider pays the driver directly (cash, UPI or as agreed). Cab Sarathi does not collect ride payments and charges no commission on rides.
  • Tolls, parking, state entry taxes and waiting charges are part of the agreement between rider and driver, so please discuss them before booking.
  • Any dispute about the fare, route or service is between the rider and the driver. We may help through our support process but are not obliged to pay or refund any amount for a ride.

7. Cancellations
